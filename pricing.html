Pricing

Simple pricing that grows with your church

Every plan includes every feature. Choose by the size of your congregation.

Included in every plan

  • Web admin and mobile app
  • Members, groups and age groups
  • Events, RSVP and QR check-in
  • Announcements and push notifications
  • Giving, expenses and reports
  • Online giving through Paynow
  • Prayer wall with moderation tools
  • Roles and permissions
  • Sermons and Bible reading plans
  • Support by email

Pricing questions

What counts as a member?

Anyone who has joined your church in the app or been added by your administrators.

Can we change plan later?

Yes. Move up when your church grows, or down if it shrinks.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Pay monthly, or pay yearly and get two months free.

How do we pay?

We invoice in US dollars. Ask us about the payment options that suit your church.

Do members pay anything?

No. The member app is free.

What is included?

Every feature, on every plan. Plans differ only by the size of your congregation.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us how big your church is and we will recommend a plan.
