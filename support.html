How do members delete their account?

In the app: Profile, then Settings, then Delete my account. See Delete your account for details.

I forgot my password.

Use Reset your password and we will email you a link.

How do I report a post?

Open the prayer or testimony and tap Report. Your church leaders review it. Reported posts are hidden for you straight away.

How do I block someone?

Tap Report, then choose Block. You can unblock in Settings, under Blocked people.

Who do I contact about a problem in the community?

The leaders of your own church moderate its community. Their email is shown in the app under Settings, Report a problem.

Which phones are supported?

iPhone and Android phones. Church leaders can use the web admin in any modern browser.
