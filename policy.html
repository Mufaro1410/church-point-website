Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are important to us

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Fun Tech Solutions ("we", "us") operates Church Point, a web admin for churches and a mobile app for their members. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. By using Church Point you agree to it, together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Each church decides which members it records and how it uses their details; we host the data and keep it safe on the church's behalf. Your church administrators can see your details. Other churches cannot.

2. What we collect

  • Profile: name, email, phone, gender, date of birth, address and profile photo that you or your church provide. Your email is your sign-in.
  • Church activity: event attendance, groups and places you serve, prayer requests, testimonies, Bible reading progress.
  • Giving records: tithes, offerings, pledges and payment references. Card or mobile-money details are handled by the payment provider, not stored by us.
  • Family links: relationships your church links (for example parent and child).
  • Device and usage: device model, app version, push-notification token and basic diagnostics.
  • Consent: the date you accepted the Terms and this policy (and, for a child, the guardian's agreement).

3. How we use it

  • Manage membership, attendance, groups, visitors and follow-up.
  • Let leaders and members communicate, and send announcements, event reminders and push notifications (you can turn push off in the app Settings).
  • Record and report giving for the church's own accounts.
  • Confirm your email and help you reset a forgotten password.
  • Keep Church Point secure, reliable and working offline on your phone.

4. Services we use

  • Supabase – sign-in, database and storage.
  • Amazon Web Services – hosting of the Church Point servers.
  • Cloudflare – website and web-app delivery.
  • Paynow – online payments and donations.
  • Twilio / email provider – SMS, WhatsApp and email messages.
  • Expo / Apple / Google – push notifications.

These providers process data only to run their service for us and have their own privacy policies.

5. Who can see your information

Authorised administrators and leaders of your own church see your details according to their role. Prayers and testimonies marked "leaders only" are visible only to leaders. We do not sell or rent personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. We disclose it only with your consent, to service providers listed above, or where the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Giving and financial records are kept by the church that received them, with your name, because the church must keep its accounts. Finance entries are voided, not deleted. If you leave a church or move to another one, your old church keeps the records it made and your new church sees only what is recorded from then on.

7. Your rights

  • See and update your profile in the app at any time (your email stays fixed because it is your sign-in).
  • Ask your church or us to correct information.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings), or email us. Your login, contact details, prayers, testimonies and family links are removed; the church keeps its giving records.
  • Turn off push notifications in the app.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and access is limited by role. Email addresses are confirmed before an account is linked to a member profile. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and tell your church administrator if you suspect misuse.

9. Children

Children may belong to their church on Church Point with an adult's guidance. For a member under 13, a parent or guardian must agree to the Terms and this policy on their behalf; we record their name and the date. We keep only what the church needs for pastoral care, children's details are visible only to church leaders, and a parent or guardian can ask the church to correct or delete a child's account at any time.

10. Account deletion

Delete your account in the app under Profile → Settings, or email churchpointorg@gmail.com with "Account deletion" in the subject. Deletion is permanent. Some records are kept as described in section 6.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and continued use means you accept the changes.
