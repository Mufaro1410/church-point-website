About

Software that serves the church

Church Point is built in Zimbabwe by Fun Tech Solutions, to give churches the tools to care for their people without drowning in paperwork.

What we believe

  • Ministry first. Administration should take less of a leader’s week, not more.
  • Honest records. Finance records are voided, never deleted, so the history can always be explained.
  • Safe for everyone. Members of every age use the app, so moderation and consent are built in.
  • Your data stays yours. We do not sell personal information. See our Privacy Policy.

Talk to us

We work closely with the churches that use Church Point. Tell us what your church needs and we will tell you honestly whether it fits.
