Features

Everything your church needs, in one place

A web admin for leaders and a mobile app for members, working from the same records.

Members & church life

Member directory

Profiles, contact details and church membership in one place.

Cell groups, ministries and courses

Create the groups your church actually uses, and assign members.

Age groups

Define your own age groups per church and see reports by age.

Bible reading plans

Publish reading plans members can follow in the app.

Events & communication

Events and RSVP

Members RSVP in the app. You see who is coming.

QR check-in

Scan a member’s code at the door to record attendance.

Reminders and push notifications

Reminders go out the day before and on the day to members who said they are coming.

Announcements

Post once and every member sees it in the app.

Sermons

Upload sermon audio so members can listen any time.

Giving & finance

Giving

Record tithes, offerings and other giving in several currencies.

Online giving

Members can give through Paynow.

Expenses

Record what the church spends and keep it apart from giving.

Reports

Per-currency financial reports, with CSV export and print.

Community & safety

Prayer requests and testimonies

A shared wall for the church family.

Moderation

Language filter, report and block, review queue and leader actions.

Leaders-only sharing

Sensitive requests go to leaders only.

Roles & permissions

Give each leader only the areas they need.
