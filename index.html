Giving

Clear books, in the currencies you use

Record giving and expenses in more than one currency, set your own rate against the US dollar, and see every report per currency. Members can give online through Paynow.

  • Giving and expenses kept separate and easy to review
  • Records are voided, never deleted, so the history stays honest
  • Export reports to CSV or print them for the church board

Per-currency reports

No mixing of dollars and local currency. Every total is shown in the currency it was given in.

Role-based access

Only the leaders you choose can see or change finance records.

A prayer wall members can trust

Prayer requests and testimonies, with a language filter, report and block tools, and a moderation queue for church leaders.

Share with leaders only

Sensitive requests can go to church leaders instead of the community feed.

Safe community

Care for the whole church family

Church Point is used by members of every age, so safety is built in: terms every member accepts, parent or guardian consent for children under 13, and leaders who can act on a report quickly.

  • Reported posts are hidden and reviewed by your church leaders
  • Leaders can remove a post or a member who breaks the rules
  • Members can delete their own account in the app

Questions

Good to know

Who is Church Point for?

Churches of any size. Pastors and administrators use the web admin to run the church, and members use the mobile app on iPhone or Android to stay connected.

Do my members have to pay?

No. The subscription is paid by the church. The member app is free to download.

Which currencies can we record?

You can record giving and expenses in more than one currency, set your own exchange rate against the US dollar, and see every report per currency.

Is our members’ data safe?

Data is stored in a managed database over encrypted connections, and each leader only sees what their role allows. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.
