1. Who these terms are for

Churches register an organisation, appoint administrators and manage their members. Members use the mobile app to join one church and use its features. A person must give accurate details and be authorised to act for the church they register.

2. Accounts

3. What churches agree to

  • Use member information only for pastoral care and church management, and respect your members' privacy.
