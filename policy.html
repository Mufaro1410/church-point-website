Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Fun Tech Solutions (“we”, “our”) provides Church Point, a tool for churches to manage members, events, giving and communication, through a mobile app and a web admin. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using Church Point you agree to it.

1. Information we collect

Information you or your church give us: name, email address, phone number, gender and date of birth where provided, church membership, group and attendance records, event RSVPs, giving and payment records, and what you post in the community (prayer requests and testimonies).

Parent or guardian details: for members under 13 we record the guardian’s name and the time consent was given.

Technical information: device type, operating system, app version and a push notification token so we can send you notifications. Payment details are handled by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers.

2. How we use it

  • To run your church’s account: members, groups, events, attendance, giving and reports.
  • To send reminders, announcements and other notifications you or your church choose.
  • To process online giving.
  • To keep the community safe and to enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To maintain and improve the app.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. Community content and moderation

Prayer requests and testimonies you post are visible to members of your church, unless you choose “Share with church leaders only”, in which case only the author and church leaders with the right permission can see them. Posts are checked by a language filter. Members can report content and block other members. Reported content is reviewed by the leaders of your church, who can remove content and remove members who break the rules.

4. Children

Members of any age may use the app. For children under 13 we ask for a parent or guardian’s consent when the child first opens the app. Parents and guardians can ask us or the church to delete a child’s account at any time (see section 8). We do not use children’s information for advertising.

5. Service providers

We use providers to deliver Church Point. They process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage.
  • Paynow: online payments.
  • Expo: delivery of push notifications.
  • Twilio: SMS or WhatsApp messages, where your church uses them.
  • Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare: hosting of our servers and websites.

6. Sharing

Your information is visible to your church’s leaders according to their roles. We share it with service providers as described above, with your consent, or when the law requires.

7. Security and retention

Data travels over encrypted connections and leaders only see what their role allows. No system is perfectly secure. We keep personal information while your account is active. Church finance records are kept (marked void, not erased) so the church’s accounts stay accurate; when an account is deleted, personal details are removed from these records where possible.

8. Your rights and deleting your account

You may access and correct your information, ask for a copy, object to communications, and delete your account.

Delete in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. Or by email: write to email with “Account deletion request” in the subject. See Delete your account.

Deletion is permanent. Requests by email are verified and completed within 30 days.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use means you accept the changes.

10. Contact

Fun Tech Solutions, Zimbabwe. Email: email. For community concerns, contact the leaders of your own church; their email is in the app under Settings.
